How did life begin? 🧪 Dr. Cyril Ponnamperuma, born in Galle in 1923, spent his career on that question: at NASA Ames, where he led the Chemical Evolution Branch, and at the University of Maryland, where he directed the Laboratory of Chemical Evolution.
